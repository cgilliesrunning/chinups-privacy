Delete your ChinUps data

In the app

  1. Open ChinUps and tap the Settings icon.
  2. Under Data, choose Delete history to remove all sessions and baseline tests but keep your current peaks, or Delete all my data to remove everything ChinUps stores for you and sign out.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Deleting the sign-in account

Your Google sign-in is shared with the Progressive Workout app by the same developer. To delete the account record itself, use Settings → Account → Delete account in Progressive Workout, or email cgilliesrunning@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will delete it within 30 days.
