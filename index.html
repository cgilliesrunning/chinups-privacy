Privacy Policy for ChinUps

Last updated: 28 September 2026

ChinUps (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a simple Android app for progressive pyramid workouts of pushups and chinups. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and your choices.

Who is responsible for your data

The app is developed and operated by Cameron Gillies. For any privacy questions or requests, contact cgilliesrunning@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to function:

  • Account identity. When you sign in with Google, we store your account identifier and email address via Firebase Authentication. This lets your progress survive reinstalls and sync across devices. The same sign-in is shared with the Progressive Workout app by the same developer.
  • Your workout data. Your current pyramid peak per exercise, baseline max tests, each workout session (date, sets and reps done, pass or fail, rest taken) and your app settings (rest duration, sound, vibration, voice). This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account.

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or advertising data, and the app contains no ads. Reminder notifications, if you enable them, are scheduled on your device and send nothing to us.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to operate the app: to run your workouts, apply the progression rules, and show your reports. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google as a service provider on our behalf. See Google’s privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while you use the app. From Settings you can delete your workout history, or delete all ChinUps data, at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Because the sign-in identity is shared with Progressive Workout, deleting the sign-in account itself is done from that app, or by emailing us. See how to delete your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
